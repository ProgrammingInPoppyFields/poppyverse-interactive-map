WELCOME TO THE POPPYVERSE

A quantum story archive for narrative inconsistency, emotional damage, and suspiciously well-organized chaos.

Enter 2D Map Enter 3D Map

Poppyverse is a long-running, experimental speculative-fiction multiverse about machines, memory, identity, intimacy, mortality, and realities that refuse to stay put.

Built one story at a time, it moves between workplace comedy, quiet grief, impossible crossovers, and the occasional branch that notices it is fictional.

Every story belongs to a universe with its own tone, characters, and corner of the lore.

Some remain comfortably inside it. Others reach across timelines, collide with alternate versions of familiar people, or become suspicious of the story containing them.

Reader note

The archive ranges from playful and tender to mature and intense. Individual content notes are provided for material that is especially direct, sustained, or graphic.
